01

The three plates

grain.png 924 × 540 · 1:1 source
The grain plate

Solves a real defect, not a mood: #05090A to #0A1012 is a four-step gradient in 8-bit, and it bands visibly on ordinary clinic monitors. A dither of fine noise removes the banding entirely.

Applied as
position: fixed; inset: 0;
mix-blend-mode: soft-light;
opacity: .045; background-size: cover;
pointer-events: none;

Not seamless — do not tile. Stretch one plate to cover. Its own base value is irrelevant under soft-light; only its variance survives.

fog-plate.png 924 × 540 · 16:9 source
The fog plate

Gives the seam an organic edge that a CSS gradient cannot: real vapour has uneven density, and that unevenness is what stops the seam reading as a ruled line. It supplements --fog-plate; it does not replace it.

Applied as
top: var(--fog-top); bottom: 0;
mix-blend-mode: screen;
opacity: .34;
mask-image: var(--fog-mask);

Sits above the filtered basemap, below the CSS plate. Masked by the same --fog-mask, so the two share one onset.

cover.png 924 × 1232 · 3:4 · three-tile stitch
The cover field

Concentric interference rings radiating from a single point of emitted warm light — an optical diffraction target, or phosphor persistence on a long exposure. It is the whole product in one image: an instrument resolving a signal out of a dark field, with the rings as the calibration and the core as the reading.

It also happens to be a single glowing amber point on cold black — which is exactly what one clinic looks like on our map. That rhyme was not prompted for, and it is the reason I kept this generation rather than refining it.

Sampled core
#D49667

Inside the glaze arc without being asked. No colour correction was applied.

Placement rule

Full bleed. No type within 180 px of the core — the cover has one focal point and the title is not it.

What failed, and what it cost
Recraft V4.1 at 2k, all three — rejected
Requires basic plan or higher. It was the better tool: it accepts a hex palette and a background colour, so the plates could have been generated inside our tokens rather than nudged toward them. Preflighted at 8 credits each; nothing was charged.
z_image, fog plate — first submission rate-limited
429 rate_limit_reached. Resubmitted unchanged after 30 s and completed. The prompt is identical to the one recorded above.
Delivery is a re-capture, not the original file
The CDN blocks cross-origin reads, so the plates were captured from the rendered image rather than downloaded. They are therefore lower resolution than the source — 924 px against 2048. Adequate for two blurred overlays; the cover was stitched from three tiles to keep its full 3:4 field. Anyone with CDN access should re-pull the originals from the job ids above.
What was never generated, and never will be

A hard rule from CLAUDE.md, carried into the guide unchanged: never generate imagery of a real clinic. Ratings and reviews here are measured data; a generated image beside them is a fabrication.
