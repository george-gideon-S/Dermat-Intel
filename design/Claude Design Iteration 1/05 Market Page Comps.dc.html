07

Degrading loudly

Three gap states — two of them real, one of them honestly labelled as not.

You asked for a row whose clinic has no coordinates. Every one of the 92 places in the 20 August snapshot has coordinates — I checked all of them against feed.json. So that row is a specified contingency, not something I can populate, and inventing a clinic to fill it would break the rule the state exists to enforce. It is drawn below with a stamp saying so. Beside it are the gaps this snapshot really does carry: a clinic with no rating captured at all, and one whose review pane never opened. run_health: partial · 9 review-pane failures · 10 card-only records.
