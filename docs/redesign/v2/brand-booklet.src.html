Typography — three faces, three jobs

Geist · every word
Trusted in person.
Invisible online.
Doto · hero numerals only 0123456789 Geist Mono · labels & data SEARCHES TRACKED · 47 REVIEWS VS 212 MEDIAN

Rules

Headlines: Geist 700, −0.02em, sentence case. Body 11–16px, 1.55 leading, ≤65ch. Doto always at roundness 100 — numerals only, never words. Micro-labels always mono, uppercase, +0.08em. The huge numeral-to-label size contrast is a brand signature — never flatten it.

Motion & charts

Motion — calm-confident

TokenValueJob
--dur-quick240msfeedback
--dur-base380msreveals, moves
--dur-slow520mscount-ups, the gate

Signatures: numerals count up · ticks draw in sequence · fields breathe (ambient). Never bounce, never linear. Reduced-motion: durations zero; the page reads fully static. Liquid-glass: nav, gate, pills, modal chrome only — never behind data.

Accessibility & misuse

Computed contrast (WCAG 2.1)

PairRatioVerdict

ink-2 / canvas5.25:1secondary ✓

Focus: 2px ink ring offset 2px. Keyboard-reachable everything. Reduced-motion first-class.

Never do these

Body copy or charts on grain fields · Doto for words · wrong triad for the meaning · hue ramps for magnitude · lime as a surface or >3 moments · pure-white canvas · flat linear gradients posing as fields · liquid-glass behind data · alarm-red · bounce easing · decorative rulers · motion that survives reduced-motion.

The closing rule: we sell precision about someone's market. An imprecise layout contradicts the product before a word is read.
