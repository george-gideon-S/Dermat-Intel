Five mesh jewels

2–4 radials over one base linear, every radial anchored outside the box so colour arrives from off-card. Three voices each: pale frame → vivid core → deep anchor, plus a tinted bloom past the edge. Our divergence from the Log App: the visibility jewel state-maps — the colour drains of green as the score falls, so a 34 can never render as reassuring.

What this overturns from v2

The canvas was never cool-tinted. Every neutral probe returned R=G=B exactly. v2's #E9EAEC canvas and #131417 ink have no measured basis → #EDEDED / #232323.
Cards are not flat white. v2 painted #FFFFFF on everything. The reference runs five rungs, and a nested card is measurably brighter than its parent (#FAFAFA inside #F3F3F3).
Weight 700 is wrong everywhere. v2 set --fw-display:700 and dot numerals at 700. Nothing in the reference exceeds 600; hero numerals sit near 320.
The score jewel is not orange. Green owns everything below y≈0.45. The blazing orange belongs to the Index jewel — v2 mis-attributed it, and its triads were 2–3× too pastel.

Calibration swatch strip

Flat, stable targets for the headless probe harness. Without it every probe lands on a gradient blend and the tolerances stop meaning anything.
